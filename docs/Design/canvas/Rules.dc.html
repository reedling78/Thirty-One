How to play

The aim

Hold three cards. Each turn, draw one from the deck or the discard and throw one. Only cards in one suit count. When you think you won't be lowest, knock — everyone else gets one more turn, then hands are shown and the lowest folds a corner of their chip.

Scoring

Ace 11 · face cards and tens 10 · the rest face value. Three of a kind, or three in a row in one suit, score 30. Ace plus two ten-cards in one suit is 31 and wins the round instantly.
