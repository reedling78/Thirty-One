Number cards carry their pips in the traditional three columns with the lower half turned, so a 7 or a 10 is recognisable by its pattern before you read the index. Aces, jacks, queens and kings all carry one large centre pip — the index letter tells them apart, and the big symbol keeps the suit readable at a glance. The back is a cream diamond lattice over a solid colour inside a white border — the classic casino back, drawn fresh. All four colourways share the one pattern, so a hidden card reads the same whichever the player picks.
