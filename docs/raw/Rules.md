


### The game
- Thirty one is a card game that used normal playing cards.
-  Each player gets dealt three cards
- The game has two piles in the middle a draw pile and a discard pile
- Each game must have at least two players

### Players hand
- You can count each cards values as a point 2 = 2 10 = 10 ect. 
- Cards must be the same suite. 
- Face cards worth 10 except the ace which is 11
- All cards must be suited to count towards the hand total
- Straight counts as thirty
- A hand can only have three cards.
